Hi, I'm **Mingyi Deng (邓明熠)**, an MStat student at the University of Hong Kong. I received my B.S. in Statistics from Renmin University of China.

I work on **LLM-based agents**, especially *interactive* and *user-centric* agents: how agents should recognize ambiguity, ask effective clarifying questions, and learn from multi-turn user feedback. I'm currently an LLM Algorithm Research Intern at **Alibaba**.

Reach me at **dengmingyi1219@163.com**.
